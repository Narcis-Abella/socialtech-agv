## Mechanistic interpretation and limits

V: The eco01 PGM has 106,533 known free 5 cm cells, about 266.3 m². The pinned [global initializer](https://github.com/ros-navigation/navigation2/blob/1.3.13/nav2_amcl/src/amcl_node.cpp#L428-L452) samples position in free space and yaw over a full circle. The pinned [differential motion model](https://github.com/ros-navigation/navigation2/blob/1.3.13/nav2_amcl/src/motion_model/differential_motion_model.cpp#L38-L103) has zero proposal noise when odometry delta is exactly zero. **I:** in a stationary S2 trial with identity odometry, repeated scans can reweight/resample existing poses but cannot explore missing positions through motion noise. Under an ideal uniform continuous approximation, only about 0.09 / 0.24 / 0.47 / 0.94 initial samples are expected within 0.30 m and 8° of one true pose at 2k / 5k / 10k / 20k particles. This is a rough coverage diagnostic, not a bound on the filter's eventual accuracy: map likelihoods can reward poses farther away and resampling behavior matters.

V: The pinned [particle-filter resampler](https://github.com/ros-navigation/navigation2/blob/1.3.13/nav2_amcl/src/pf/pf.c#L1854-L1911) computes the random-pose injection probability from `w_fast/w_slow`. With both recovery coefficients at their default zero, the running weight estimates remain equal and no recovery poses are injected after global initialization. This mechanism reinforces the stationary coverage limitation. The upstream code comments suggest `recovery_alpha_fast=0.1`, `recovery_alpha_slow=0.001` as a possible recovery setting; v28 tried those values on DEVELOPMENT with 5,000 particles. V: all 29 updates were scored, but no pose passed the fixed gate. Post-run scoring found exactly the same final wrong mode as the no-recovery v20 control, 16.72 m / 10.88° from reference. The exact same maximum concentration (0.6016) does not prove that recovery failed internally; the injected-pose count is not exposed by these logs. A future claim about injection effectiveness would require direct particle ancestry or a controlled source-instrumented run. V: doubling `max_beams` from 180 to 360 in v30 also scored all 29 updates but ended in the same wrong mode; concentration fell from 0.6016 to 0.4270. This one-window comparison gives no evidence that more beams fix the global search.

**NV-P:** a calibrated physical sensor height/base extrinsic, a complete particle-count comparison, AMCL tuning on multiple windows/datasets, and independent final data with >=30 spatial blocks per cell. The current same-run geometry diagnostic is a best case. No Point 7 method has been run. The exact provisional conclusion remains open until diverse FOV and cross-dataset trials are scored.
