## Approved scenarios and priority

**V, requirement from Narcís via user, 2026-10-07:** maximize correct localization and minimize false acceptance; licensing is secondary. Evaluate three distinct scenarios:

| Scenario | Approved observation/motion scope | Research implication |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | Stationary robot, one scan | **I:** strict single-observation test; IMU/gravity initialization may precede it, but cannot silently include earlier LiDAR scans. **NV/P:** define a scan's duration/packet boundary and permitted frontend warm-up at point 4. |
| S2 | Stationary robot, multiple accumulated scans | **I:** improves Livox coverage/density without assuming translation. No intentional rotation: that is movement and belongs to S3. Repeated observations share geometry and errors; they are not independent votes. **NV/P:** accumulation duration/limits. |
| S3 | Free short motion, always within 3 m of starting point, multiple scans joined with odometry | **I:** new viewpoints may disambiguate hypotheses. The 3 m limit is radial excursion, not total path length; returning to start does not erase an excursion. Requires trustworthy relative odometry and a local motion policy before global pose acceptance. **NV/P:** timeout/path budget and admissible motion policy. |

**I:** existing replayed moving scans cannot be relabelled S2 by freezing their timestamps. A batch accumulator can consume one cloud under S1, but a frontend that used several LiDAR scans is not strict S1 merely because its final API receives one cloud. No effectiveness guarantee follows from the allowed 3 m excursion.

## Expected fit and resource risks

**I:** repeated corridors can produce equivalent hypotheses for every geometry-only family. A low residual, many inliers, a sharp particle distribution or a high synthetic posterior cannot certify the true location. Partial overlap particularly challenges scan-to-full-map correspondence methods and descriptor transfer; submap retrieval has preparation/storage costs that must not be omitted later.

**V:** KISS, small_gicp, Quatro/TEASER, classical Scan Context/BTC, hdl, als_ros, PRISM, Cartographer, OFL, MapClosures and emcl2 have CPU paths; 3D-BBS offers CPU and CUDA paths; GeoTransformer, BEVPlace++ and RING++ examined recipes use PyTorch/CUDA. RING++ is learning-free despite its GPU dependencies. **I:** feature/clique graphs, descriptor databases, voxel pyramids and learned tensors produce different memory peaks. **NV/P:** all NX costs, feasible GPU configuration and thermals. Published PC/synthetic timings are not NX estimates. Shared preprocessing/LIO, map/database preparation and failed attempts must be included in later cost accounting.

**V:** a scan or scan pair can drive KISS/Quatro/TEASER, BBS and OFL without a preceding map-frame pose. BTC's published pipeline accumulates registered scans; PRISM's verifier and lidar_localization_ros2's moving-start remedy need time/odometry. Cartographer retains active local submaps even in pure localization. **I:** movement can improve observability but also makes delayed pose handoff harder. S1/S2/S3 are now approved; their effectiveness and practical time limits are unmeasured.

**I:** our upward-looking sensor can lose its strongest returns when matched against ceiling-free map_clean.ply. Full world.ply requires its documented map-frame transform and handling of outliers. Mount changes can invalidate descriptor/visibility assumptions. **NV/P:** exact representation choice and processing belong to subsequent points, not this research closure.

## New source checks and transferable implementation findings

### MapClosures: prior-map adaptation, not a ready boot localizer

**V:** [MC] paper uses odometry-registered local 3D maps and BEV density-image features; relative 2D alignment is verified using RANSAC. Current API `GetTopKClosures()` returns transforms and inlier counts without an initial map pose (`MapClosures.hpp:49-99`). Source snapshot `434b41fb33c85c29bc9fa4b453a1ef651fed7d61` was updated 2026-10-06; the separate ICRA2024 tag reproduces the paper, not this later API. Core C++ uses Eigen, OpenCV, Sophus and HBST; Python pipeline also depends on KISS-ICP. No GPU requirement in the inspected core; no live Jazzy/Jetson validation found. ROS2 MCAP/bag input readers are not ROS2 localization-node support.

**V:** `MapClosures.cpp:79-98,153-170` matches descriptors against themselves, retains a descriptor only when its second-nearest self-match has Hamming distance **greater than 35**, then queries HBST. This removes locally self-similar features, rather than proving that a whole corridor is unique. `:238-265` adds the query into the database, skips the last three map IDs, and ranks surviving candidates by RANSAC inlier count. `GroundAlign.cpp:70-128,157-182` estimates alignment from low-lying planar voxels; no ground samples returns identity.

**I:** preload overlapping prior-map tiles in known map coordinates, query an odometry-built local submap, compose each tile's known map transform with the correctly directed closure transform, refine in 3D and confirm on later scans. This reuses the registration mechanism beyond same-session loop closure; no map-frame startup pose is necessary. **NV/P:** freezing the reference database, avoiding query contamination, making every prior tile searchable despite the last-three-ID exclusion, transform direction tests and density normalization. Public top-K currently mutates its database; this is not a turnkey read-only map-query API.

**I:** pruning could lower false correspondence risk in repetitive corridors, but also remove most useful features. A stationary cloud or movement within 3 m might not yield a discriminative local map. Map-clean excludes floor/ceiling and is voxelized; aligning ground and comparing point-density images against differently accumulated live clouds require representation work. Full, levelled world.ply may be more suitable, subject to outliers. **NV/P:** indoor Mid-360 overlap/ground observability and false accepts; no source establishes success under our 3 m constraint.

## Proposed staged architecture — design only

**I, proposal:** use a common observation/frame contract and retain several spatially distinct hypotheses until acceptance or explicit ambiguous/rejected output. This proposes stages, not K values, windows, thresholds or point-4 metrics.

| Stage | Proposed behavior (I) | Candidate mechanisms (V where already established above) / unresolved parts |
|---|---|---|
| Prepare observations | S1 one query cloud; S2 stationary accumulation; S3 timestamped deskewed clouds joined in a common local odom frame. Prepare consistent levelled prior-map representations and georeferenced tiles/scan databases | Existing FAST-LIO2 is the first frontend to reuse; changing to Point-LIO introduces another variable. **NV/P:** boot/gravity readiness, actual stationary windows and drift. Never use Voxel-SLAM reference trajectory to accumulate query scans. |
| Generate top-K hypotheses | Search globally without a seed; preserve distinct places/yaws, scores, source scan stamp, provenance and alternatives | BBS 2D LL/PRISM and MapClosures expose top-K. hdl engines, SC/BTC/STD and RING retrieval provide candidate mechanisms. KISS/Quatro/TEASER on prior-map tiles, 3D-BBS multiple peaks and BEV FAISS top-K need adapters; do not pretend single-result APIs already do this. |
| Refine each hypothesis | 3D small_gicp/GICP/NDT, restricted to hypothesis-compatible visible map region; retain alternatives rather than merging all into one local optimum | small_gicp/hdl/LL refinement; KISS or Quatro could refine correspondence hypotheses. **NV/P:** overlap/visibility model, suitable ceiling-inclusive cloud, gravity and sensor/base extrinsics. Convergence alone is insufficient. |
| Verify multi-scan / motion | Evaluate additional scans not used to fit the pose, geometric support/free-space conflicts, overlap, degeneracy and relative support of alternatives; compare odometry-consistent map->odom hypotheses | PRISM posterior/ambiguous mode, LL compensated consensus, als_ros reliability and emcl2 penetration rate are transferable mechanisms. **I:** S2 adds density/repeatability; S3 offers new viewpoints. **NV/P:** calibrated gates and independence assumptions; 3 m can remain ambiguous. |
| Accept / reject / remain ambiguous | Accept only supported, temporally consistent, distinct-enough hypotheses under a later approved policy; report rejection/ambiguity when evidence is insufficient | Preserve score, inliers, residual, visible support, runner-up alternatives, timing and odometry health for later diagnostics. **NV/P:** all decision thresholds and treatment of no-accept outcomes belong to point 4. Evaluation reference errors must not enter the online gate. |
| Deliver to AMCL | Transport accepted map pose to current timestamp; convert sensor/body pose to levelled base_footprint; publish initialpose with justified uncertainty; verify later AMCL convergence | Existing pose_eval/frame helpers and AMCL interface. **I:** AMCL becomes the sole map->odom publisher in this architecture. A continuous 3D localizer that publishes this TF must be stopped/suppressed for handoff. **NV/P:** concrete lifecycle/covariance policy and end-to-end validation, not implemented. |

**I, timestamp convention:** with `T_A_B` mapping coordinates from B into A, a sensor-pose hypothesis implies `T_map_odom = T_map_sensor(t) * inverse(T_odom_sensor(t))`; propagate with current local odometry and known extrinsics for the handoff. If registering a submap already expressed in odom, its output may already be `T_map_odom`: applying that formula again would double-transform it. Validate transform direction and scan/odom timestamps before any candidate comparison.

**I:** prioritize effectiveness by retaining ambiguity and acquiring informative observations, rather than accepting the first low residual. S3 local moves must honor the approved 3 m radial envelope without relying on an already-correct global pose. **NV/P:** motion controller, collision checking, local odometry error margin and time budget. No movement is executed here. Same-geometry multi-scan agreement can consistently confirm the wrong corridor; an unresolved case must remain unresolved.
